Mission Projects

Opportunities at our church to make a difference for Jesus Christ!

Mid-South Food Bank

Our Deacons support the Mid-South Food Bank through personal online donations. Donations may be made in honor of Rosemark First Presbyterian Church so the church can receive credit toward a matching donation. More information: midsouthfoodbank.org.

One Great Hour of Sharing

You can give online to “One Great Hour of Sharing.” Please keep a record of your gift to share with our church treasurer so the church can earn a matching gift.

Presbyterian Special Offerings

Throughout the year our congregation participates in the denomination's special offerings, including the Pentecost Offering, the Peace & Global Witness Offering, the Christmas Joy Offering, and the Presbyterian Women's Birthday and Thank Offerings.

Northaven Elementary School

We have “adopted” Northaven School, which is greatly challenged with regard to funding and resources. Believing that Jesus loves children, we reach out with love in His name. Through our “Socks for Tots” effort we collect new socks and underwear for boys and girls (Toddler PK through Grade 5), as many children come to school in cold weather without socks.

YWCA Memphis Family Shelter

Rosemark FPC helped found this shelter, which provides safety, food, and housing for women and children who are victims of domestic violence. We collect donated items, including baby supplies, cleaning supplies, household items, and health and personal-care items. A Thanksgiving food offering is received once a year. Contact: Rosemark Board of Deacons.

Rosemark Country Fair

At this annual event, which our church helps sponsor, Rosemark FPC runs a booth to introduce visitors to our church and our ministries in the community and around the world. We prepare and distribute gift bags with favors for children and brochures for adults. For more information and to participate, contact our Growth Team Co-Chairs. Entry to the fair is free!

Backpack Sunday

We collect school supplies for students at Northaven School.
