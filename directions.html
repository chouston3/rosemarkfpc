Directions

The First Presbyterian Church of Rosemark, Tennessee.

Our address is:
First Presbyterian Church of Rosemark
8592 East Kerrville-Rosemark Road
Rosemark, TN 38053

The First Presbyterian Church of Rosemark is about half a mile west of the four-way stop sign at the intersection of Rosemark Road and East Kerrville-Rosemark Road.

It is just around the corner from the old cotton gin and up the street from the Brighton Bank.

It is just south and around the corner from Tipton Rosemark Academy.

It is about a mile and a half northwest of the flashing yellow light at the intersection of Rosemark Road and the Austin Peay Highway (Route 14).
