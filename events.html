Events

All are welcome to join us! Below are our regular weekly and seasonal gatherings.

Sunday Worship

Sunday Worship is normally at 11:00 am in the church sanctuary, with childcare available. Our nursery is on the ground level of the new building.

Sunday Bible Class

Sunday Bible Class is normally at 10:00 am in the Fellowship Hall of the new building. Led by a rotation of church members as teachers, all are welcome.

Presbyterian Women's Bible Study

Led by Mrs. Elizabeth Houston, this study includes pot-luck snacks and desserts. It is normally held on the first or second Tuesday of the month during the school year at 11:00 am in our Fellowship Hall.

Wednesday Bible Study (seasonal)

Led by Dr. Houston. Dates to be announced.

Fifth Sunday Fellowship Dinner

Our Fellowship Dinner is normally held every fifth Sunday at 12:15 pm in the Fellowship Hall of our new building. The YWCA Memphis Family Shelter Wish List offering is received at these dinners.

Communion

Sunday Worship with Communion is normally celebrated quarterly at 11:00 am, including World Communion Sunday in October.

Hanging of the Greens

Join us to make the sanctuary beautiful for Christmas!

Christmas in the Country

A holiday celebration with guest musicians, held the first Sunday in December at 2:15 pm in the sanctuary.
