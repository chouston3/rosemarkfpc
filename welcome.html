Welcome

Welcome to the Website of the First Presbyterian Church in Rosemark, Tennessee.

As you explore this website, I hope you will develop the desire to visit our church in person. I know that you will enjoy exploring our many ministries and discovering what God is doing in and through us.

Come visit to experience our worship: to lift your voice in songs of praise, to lift your heart in prayers of thanksgiving, and to lift your spirit in gratitude for God’s love.

Come visit to experience the creative joy and personal fulfillment of building up the church as the Body of Christ: to develop and use your gifts for witness, compassion, and service to a needy and hurting world.

Come visit to experience God’s blessings as part of our church family. We are dedicated to grow in our knowledge and love of Jesus Christ.

All this awaits and more! I hope to see you soon. Please be sure to say, “Hi!”

— Dr. Horace Houston
